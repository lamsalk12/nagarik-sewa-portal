# Code of conduct

नागरिक सेवा पोर्टल सबैका लागि हो। यहाँ योगदान गर्ने सबैसँग सम्मानपूर्वक व्यवहार गरौं।
This project is for everyone in Nepal. Everyone who takes part should be treated with respect.

## Expected
- Be kind and patient, especially with first-time contributors and people writing in their second language.
- Nepali, English, Maithili, Bhojpuri, Nepal Bhasa and other languages of Nepal are all welcome in issues and reviews.
- Disagree about the work, not the person. Back corrections with an official source.
- Keep this project non-political: describe services, not parties or politicians.

## Not acceptable
- Insults, harassment, threats, or attacks based on caste, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexuality, disability, region, language or politics.
- Posting anyone's personal details (citizenship, passport or phone numbers, addresses), including your own.
- Spam, advertising, or links to unofficial sites presented as government sites.

## What happens
Maintainers may edit, hide or delete comments, close issues or pull requests, and block people who break these rules.
To report a problem, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment, or contact a maintainer through their GitHub profile.
Reports are handled privately.
